Privacy notice

This notice, provided under Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the ‘GDPR’), explains how personal data are processed when you visit this website, send an enquiry or stay at the A spasso con Daisy · Chianti Home apartments. In short: the site sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics, and the data you send with an enquiry are used only to reply and to manage your booking.

If you have any questions about this notice or wish to exercise your rights, please write to info@aspassocondaisy.com.

What data we process

Browsing the site. This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA, part of Microsoft). To deliver the pages, GitHub receives each visitor’s IP address and logs it for security purposes. The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics and, apart from the Airbnb content described below, loads no third-party resources: fonts and images are hosted on the site itself.

Cookie preferences. A single technical item, daisy-consent, is stored in your browser, containing your cookie choices and the date you made them. It stays on your device and is not sent to the controller.

Third-party content. Only with your consent does the site load the Airbnb listing for each apartment: in that case, Airbnb receives your IP address and may set its own cookies, including profiling cookies. The availability calendar, by contrast, only shows booked dates, synchronised from the Airbnb calendar by an automatic process on GitHub: it contains no guest data and sets no cookies. The ‘Open in Google Maps’ link loads nothing from Google unless you click it.

Booking enquiries. The form on the page does not send or store any data on the site: it opens your own WhatsApp or email app with a pre-filled message, and you decide whether to send it. If you do, the controller receives the data it contains: your name, arrival and departure dates, number of guests, apartment and type of stay, whether you are travelling with a dog (with its name and size) and your message, as well as the phone number or email address you write from.

Your stay. For guests who stay with us, the controller also processes the data needed to manage the booking and to meet legal obligations: guests’ personal details and identity document data, which must be reported to the police (Questura) through the Alloggiati Web service; where applicable, the data required for the tourist tax and for tourism statistics; and tax and accounting data relating to payment. If you book through Airbnb, the controller receives from Airbnb the data needed to manage your booking; Airbnb also processes them as an independent controller under its own privacy policy.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Site security (IP addresses logged by GitHub): legitimate interest in protecting the site against abuse and cyber attacks (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Storing your cookie choices: compliance with legal obligations on consent (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR); as a technical tool, it does not require consent (Article 122 of Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003, the ‘Privacy Code’).
  • Displaying the Airbnb listing: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Article 122 of the Privacy Code), which you may withdraw at any time.
  • Replying to enquiries and managing your booking and stay: steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract, and performance of the contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Legal obligations, such as reporting guests to the police (Article 109 of the Italian Consolidated Law on Public Security, TULPS), the tourist tax and tourism statistics where applicable, and tax and accounting requirements: compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

The controller does not carry out any profiling and does not take decisions based solely on automated processing.

Do you have to provide your data?

Providing data in a booking enquiry is optional, but without them we cannot reply or arrange your stay. The identity data requested on arrival, however, are required by law: without them, we cannot accommodate you.

How long we keep data

  • Booking enquiries: for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and, if no booking follows, no longer than 12 months.
  • Bookings and stays: for the duration of the relationship; tax and accounting records for 10 years, as required by law.
  • Reports to the authorities (Alloggiati Web, tourist tax, statistics): for the period required by the applicable rules.
  • Cookie preferences (daisy-consent): 6 months, after which you will be asked again.
  • IP addresses logged by GitHub: for the periods set out in GitHub’s privacy statement.

After these periods, data are deleted or anonymised.

Who may receive the data

Your data are not made public. Only to the extent necessary, they may be received by:

  • technical service providers: GitHub (website hosting), the controller’s email provider and WhatsApp (part of Meta), if you choose to write via that app;
  • professionals who assist the controller, such as an accountant;
  • public authorities, where required by law: for example the police via Alloggiati Web, the municipality for the tourist tax where applicable, and the tax authorities.

Airbnb, for the listing widget and for bookings made on its platform, and Google, if you open the Google Maps link, process data as independent controllers under their own policies: see Airbnb’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

Transfers outside the European Union

Some providers, such as GitHub (part of Microsoft) and WhatsApp (part of Meta), belong to groups based in the United States and may process data outside the European Union. In these cases, transfers are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, recognised as adequate by the European Commission in its decision of 10 July 2023 (Article 45 GDPR), or on standard contractual clauses approved by the Commission (Article 46 GDPR). You may ask the controller for more information on the safeguards in place.

Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 22 GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access your data and obtain a copy of them;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data rectified;
  • have your data erased, unless they must be kept by law;
  • restrict processing;
  • receive the data you have provided in a structured, commonly used format (portability);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest.

You may also withdraw your consent to third-party content at any time via the ‘Cookie preferences’ link at the bottom of every page. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

To exercise your rights, simply write to info@aspassocondaisy.com: you will receive a reply without undue delay and, as a rule, within one month.

Right to lodge a complaint

If you believe that the processing of your data infringes the GDPR, you may lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it). If you live or work in another EU country, you may also contact the supervisory authority of that country.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated, for example if the services used by the site or the law change. The version in force is always the one published on this page. Last updated: 11/11/2026.
