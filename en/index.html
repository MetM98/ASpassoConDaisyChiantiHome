Chianti all around you

Hills, vineyards and timeless villages

To leave the stresses of everyday life behind, you need only discover the enchanting places around us: hills clothed in vineyards that give rise to great wines, dotted with centuries-old olive trees whose oil is prized the world over. From the greenery rise fortified hamlets, little villages and castles, rich in culture and in traditions that reach back over a thousand years.

  • The park of the mastodons

    Just below the house lies the park where, some 2.5 million years ago, the mastodons of Sambuca roamed – elephant-like giants whose presence is attested by a palate with two molars, now kept in a museum. It is one of the most important natural sites in Chianti, with heirloom plants and fruit trees and places to gather.

  • Along the River Pesa

    A route of great scenic beauty, where nature and spirituality meet, in the footsteps of Saint John Gualbert.

  • Badia a Passignano

    Saint John Gualbert, born near Tavarnelle in the late tenth century and founder of the Vallombrosan Order, lived and now rests in the Abbey of San Michele Arcangelo.

  • Villages and castles

    Fortified villages and castles rising from the greenery, guardians of art, history and age-old traditions.

At your service

The heart of Chianti Classico, up close

In this corner of Chianti you will feel at home, with the privilege of activities and services reserved for the most discerning guests.

01

Art that changes with the seasons

In tribute to the artistic treasures of Florence, the rooms of A spasso con Daisy are home to paintings, sculpture, ceramics and photographs, in displays renewed with every season.

02

Among the olive trees

Sun-kissed hills 450 metres above sea level: a land made special by the fruit it bears. We will walk with you among the trees that teach the art of patience, up to the dense canopy where the olives hide, and you will discover how daily care turns into colour, flavour and fragrance.

03

In the vineyards and the cellar

If you wish, you can walk among the vines and see how the soil changes with the wine it yields. In a small cellar – a temple of ancient rituals and modern technology – past, present and future live in harmony.

04

Bread from ancient grains

Our chef will teach you to bake bread with our own organic flours, milled from ancient grains. At the end of the lesson you will savour the treats you have made, fresh from the oven, with a good local craft beer.

05

Days on horseback

Magnificent horses can make your days with us truly special, with rides, lessons and moments of genuine connection.
