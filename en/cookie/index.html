Cookie policy

This page explains which cookies and similar technologies are used on the A spasso con Daisy · Chianti Home website and how to manage your preferences. In short: the site sets no cookies of its own, and Airbnb content is loaded only if you choose to allow it.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites save in your browser and read again on later visits. Other technologies, such as the browser’s localStorage, can store information in a similar way and are subject to the same rules.

Technical tools, which the site needs in order to work, do not require consent. All others, including profiling tools and those set by third parties, may only be used with the user’s consent, as required by Article 122 of the Italian Privacy Code and the Garante’s Guidelines of 10 June 2021.

Cookies and tools used on this site

The site sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics. It stores a single technical item in your browser; third-party content is loaded only with your consent.

The availability calendar only shows booked dates and sets no cookies. As explained in the privacy notice, GitHub, which hosts the site, logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

Third-party content: Airbnb

For each apartment (Il Bilocale and Il Monolocale), the site can display the Airbnb widget with its listing. The widget is loaded from Airbnb’s servers only after you consent to the ‘Third-party content’ category. Until then, a box with a ‘Show content’ button appears in its place: by pressing it, you consent to the Airbnb content being loaded.

Once the widget is loaded, Airbnb receives your IP address and some technical data about your device, and may set its own cookies, including profiling cookies. Airbnb acts as an independent controller: we have no control over these cookies and no access to the data they collect. For more information, see Airbnb’s privacy policy and cookie policy.

External links: Google Maps, WhatsApp and email

The ‘Open in Google Maps’ button is a simple link: nothing is loaded from Google unless you click it. Once Google Maps opens, Google’s privacy policy and cookie settings apply.

Likewise, the send buttons on the booking form open WhatsApp or your email app without setting any cookies on the site.

Managing your preferences

On your first visit, a banner appears with three buttons: ‘Accept all’, ‘Reject’ and ‘Customise’, to choose individual categories. Closing the banner with the X rejects all non-essential tools: only technical ones remain active.

No boxes are pre-ticked, and scrolling the page does not count as consent. If you reject, the site works normally: the Airbnb listing is simply not shown.

Your choice is remembered for 6 months, after which you will be asked again. You can change it or withdraw your consent at any time via the ‘Cookie preferences’ link at the bottom of every page.

Deleting cookies and site data in your browser

You can delete cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings, including the choice stored by this site. If you do, the banner will appear again on your next visit. Here is where to find the option in the most common browsers (menu names may vary slightly between versions):

  • Chrome: Settings › Privacy and security › Delete browsing data (Google guide)
  • Firefox: Settings › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data › Clear Data (Mozilla guide)
  • Safari: on a Mac, Safari › Settings › Privacy › Manage Website Data; on iPhone and iPad, Settings › Safari › Clear History and Website Data (Apple guide)
  • Edge: Settings › Privacy, search and services › Clear browsing data (Microsoft guide)

In the same settings you can also block third-party cookies.
